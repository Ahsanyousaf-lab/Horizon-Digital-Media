LEGAL GUIDELINES

Terms & Conditions of Service

These terms serve as the standard operational guidelines and should be adapted with local legal counsel prior to binding commercial engagement.

01. Scope of Services

Horizon Digital Media provides creative post-production, digital asset design, video editing, direct-response UGC/CGI ad creation, prompt engineering, and custom web development.

02. Client Materials

Clients are responsible for providing all necessary raw footage, brand guidelines, audio files, copy, and product references.

03. Payment

Standard projects require a 50% advance retainer before production commences, with the remaining 50% due upon presentation of preview.

04. Revisions

Deliverables include up to two (2) rounds of reasonable revisions focused on timing, audio balance, color adjustments, and copy corrections.

05. Delivery & Approval

Previews are rendered and hosted via secure cloud review links (Frame.io, Google Drive, or Figma).

06. Rush Delivery

Turnaround requests in 24–48 hours require a flat express fee ($100) and 100% advance payment.

07. Intellectual Property

Upon full receipt of payment, all final produced commercial media assets transfer to the client.

08. Confidentiality

We routinely sign standard bilateral non-disclosure agreements for unreleased products and proprietary marketing roadmaps.

09. Cancellation

If a project is cancelled by the client after work has commenced, the initial deposit covers allocated production hours.

10. Limitation & Results

External platform algorithms and offer economics dictate market outcomes; specific ROAS cannot be legally guaranteed.
